# How to film your room

The reconstruction needs **parallax**: the camera has to move through the room. Standing still
and turning gives only a panorama, which cannot be turned into 3D.

## Before you start

- Lights on, curtains open. Even light, no flicker. Avoid direct sun patches and mirrors.
- Remove people and pets; don't move anything while filming.
- Phone in **landscape**, **4K, 30 fps**. Lock exposure and focus if your camera app allows it.
- Wipe the lens.

## What to do (about 2–3 minutes)

1. **Walk the walls.** Go once around the room, close to the walls, camera pointing at the
   *opposite* side so every wall and corner is seen from a distance.
2. **Second lap, different height.** Same route, but hold the phone low (chest) for lap one and
   high (head) or tilted down for lap two, so floors and furniture tops are covered.
3. **Centre sweep.** Stand in the middle and turn slowly through 360° **while taking two steps
   sideways**, then once more in the other direction.
4. **Close-ups of what matters.** Slowly circle your sofa, table and other furniture you want to
   move later — about 1 m away, all sides.
5. **Scale.** Film a tape measure on the floor or an open door (door height) for about three seconds.
   You enter this measurement once in klecks to get real metres.

## Rules of thumb

- Move **slowly**; blurry frames will be dropped automatically (planned, `capture.min_sharpness`).
- Every surface should be seen from at least two different positions.
- Do not point at blank white walls only — include furniture, corners and edges in every shot.
- Keep one continuous take. Don't cut and don't zoom.

## After filming

Copy the video to your PC and drag it onto the klecks window (or click the drop zone).
Planned for phase 2: klecks reports if a corner is missing so you can film just that part again.
